About Prince P. Cardines

Hello, I'm Prince P. Cardines! I'm a college student and someone who feels most alive on a basketball court or halfway up a mountain trail. I love learning new things, meeting new people, and turning every little adventure into a story.

Basketball is where I learned teamwork, discipline and how to bounce back after a missed shot. Whether it is a quick game with friends or a serious match, the court is my happy place.

Away from the court I love the outdoors: hiking up trails, camping under the stars and travelling to new places. Every adventure teaches me patience, gratitude, and that the best views come after the hardest climbs.
